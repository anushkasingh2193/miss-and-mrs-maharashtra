About

Beauty, talent and empowerment — together

The platform

A contest format that celebrates beauty beyond appearances — redefining grace, strength and individuality for women across the state. Not just a pageant: a journey of self-discovery, transformation and empowerment.

Founded in Nagpur by Mrs. Zoya Siraj Sheikh and Mr. Siraj Sheikh under Kara Zoya Pvt Ltd, the pageant was built to give Maharashtra's women a stage of national standard without asking them to leave the state to find it. Two seasons in, it has crowned titleholders in both categories and sent them onward to Mrs. India Supranational and Women of the Universe.

Our mission is to honour diversity, highlight talent and empower women to embrace their dreams with confidence. Women from all walks of life are warmly invited to step forward and express their true selves.

Every contestant — crowned or not — leaves with grooming from industry mentors, a professional portfolio, media exposure and a network of women who understand the work it takes.
