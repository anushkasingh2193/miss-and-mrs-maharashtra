# Miss & Mrs. Hero CTA Design

## Goal

Replace the generic secondary homepage hero label with a short, specific contestant-entry call to action.

## Approved design

- Keep the existing two-button hero layout, styling, responsive behavior, and navigation destinations unchanged.
- Keep the primary CTA as `Junior India Live Voting`.
- Change only the secondary CTA label from `Register Now` to `Enter Miss & Mrs.`.
- Preserve the secondary CTA route to the registration page.

## Acceptance criteria

- The homepage hero visibly renders `Enter Miss & Mrs.`.
- The old `Register Now` hero label is absent.
- Clicking the secondary CTA still navigates to `register`.
- Existing tests and the production build pass.
